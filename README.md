
## 🚀 Project Overview

Care Connect solves a real-world problem where usable items are wasted due to lack of time, awareness, or accessibility of donation systems.

This platform allows users to:
- Donate items easily
- Contribute money securely
- Schedule doorstep pickup
- Track donation history in real time

---

## 🧠 Real-World Problem

Millions of usable items are discarded every year because:

- People are too busy to donate physically  
- No simple pickup system exists  
- Donation processes are complicated  
- Lack of transparency in impact tracking

## 💡 Solution

Care Connect provides a **one-stop donation system** where users can:

- 📦 Donate unused items (Clothes, Books, Toys, Food, etc.)
- 🚚 Schedule doorstep pickup in seconds  
- 💰 Donate money via Razorpay (Test Mode)  
- 📊 Track donation history and impact  
- 📱 Get instant notifications via EmailJS / WhatsApp
